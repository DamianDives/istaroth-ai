The AP Bottleneck

Weeks of Manual Cross-Checking & Price Drift

Accounts payable teams process hundreds of vendor invoices monthly. Finance associates manually compare PDF numbers against purchase order PDFs and warehouse Goods Receipt Notes (GRNs) on paper.

Unauthorized 2% rate hikes, freight surcharges, and incorrect GST classifications slip through. Invoices get paid twice or delayed for 45 days, causing vendor disputes and loss of cash discounts.

The LedgerFlow Engine

Autonomous 3-Way Deterministic Matching

LedgerFlow automatically ingests vendor invoices from email or portal uploads. It extracts every line item, HSN code, unit rate, and tax calculation with 99.8% precision.

It pulls the corresponding PO from ERP and physical receipt from the warehouse. If all three match within pre-configured tolerance thresholds, it auto-creates the accounting voucher and prepares the bank payout file.
