An AI that answers calls, books appointments, and handles support — naturally.

▾

They're not IVR menus with press-1-for-this. They hold actual conversations, understand context, and only pass to a human when truly needed.

  • Natural, low-latency voice conversations
  • Inbound call handling (support, bookings, FAQs)

  • CRM integration — logs every call automatically
  • Works across English, Hindi, and regional languages
  • 24/7 availability with zero staffing overhead
