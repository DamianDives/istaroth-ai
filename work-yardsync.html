The Yard Bottleneck

75-Minute Gate Delays & Forged Slips

Manufacturing plants receive 100+ trucks daily. Drivers arrive with paper delivery challans that are dog-eared, ink-stained, or illegible. Security personnel write down details by hand in physical registers.

Operators then re-type weights from the weighbridge terminal into spreadsheets. Discrepancies between vendor-claimed weight and scale weight lead to bitter disputes and inventory leakage.

The YardSync Engine

Sub-90 Second Autonomous Gate Clearance

Truck drivers send a WhatsApp message with their delivery challan as they approach. Computer vision extracts the PO number, vendor name, material line items, and quantities instantly.

As the vehicle rolls onto the weighbridge, YardSync captures gross weight directly over RS-232, checks for tolerance limits against the PO, and auto-prints or texts a digital QR gate pass in under 90 seconds.
