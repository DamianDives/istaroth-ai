The Industry Challenge

The ₹1.8 Cr "Cheapest Bid" Scope Trap

In commercial construction, preconstruction teams manage dozens of trade packages—from HVAC and electrical to structural glazing. Every subcontractor submits proposals in different PDF formats, riddled with obscure exclusions and fine print.

Because estimators are pressed against submission deadlines, bids are often awarded based on bottom-line numbers. Six months into project execution, variation orders and missing scopes wipe out contractor profit margins.

The Istaroth Solution

Autonomous Precon Leveling & Intelligence

BidIntel ingests vendor quote PDFs, structural BOQs, and addenda. It extracts every line item, normalizes terms across all bidders, and detects excluded taxes, unquoted freight, and omitted testing protocols.

The platform equips estimators with an interactive AI copilot that cites exact PDF page numbers, calculates normalized true costs, and prepares board-ready bid leveling spreadsheets in minutes.
